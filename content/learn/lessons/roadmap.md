These lessons go through the fundamentals of computing in small weekly steps —
what the machine actually does, then how the software we write turns into what
the machine runs. Each lesson is one idea: a plain explanation, a small thing to
try, and a source to read further.

The order follows whatever is being learned that week; earlier lessons stay
linked as reference. Shared lessons get their own page under
[the lessons track](@/learn/lessons/_index.md).
